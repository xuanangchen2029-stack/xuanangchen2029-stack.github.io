BEAMbot

Bistable Electromagnetically Actuated Modular robots

How much controllable locomotion can emerge when electronics selects where and when bistable cells snap?

Each cell is a vacuum-formed PETG shell with laser-cut flexures, permanent magnets, a coil and a mechanical stop. Its geometry sets the force response. Bistability stores the state and elastic energy without holding current, and a short pulse triggers the snap. Across modules, the choice of which cells snap and when turns discrete states into whole-body motion. The tethered three-module robot moves forward and turns this way.

My roleI developed the thermoforming and post-form laser-cutting process for the curved bistable cells. I also worked on magnet–coil switching, built single- and multi-module prototypes and their drive electronics, and ran the mechanical and switching characterization.

Open questions
  • How can shape program mechanics? Calibrated FEA and repeated mechanical tests will link dome shape, flexure pattern and crease condition to the stable states and actuation requirements.
  • How do arrangement and timing compose motion? Placement, orientation, activation order and phase will be varied to find repeatable translation and turning.
  • How can actuation get lighter? Off-center magnets and multilayer flexible-PCB coils will be weighed against switching force, pulse energy, heating and mass, working toward untethered modules.
  • How can modules change capability? Interchangeable mechanical cells and electronic “lids” could allow local control with shared power.

With Paul Ducarme and Lucia Stein-Montalvo · PI: Nivedita Arora · First author Manuscript in preparation

LeafBreath

A ~$50, leaf-resident instrument that makes photosynthesis observable

Research-grade gas-exchange analyzers cost tens of thousands of dollars and need an operator to hold a cuvette on one leaf at a time. LeafBreath instead seals a small thermoformed chamber onto an intact leaf and measures how fast the leaf changes the air inside. Net assimilation and transpiration both come from the CO₂ and humidity slopes. With no pump, scrubbers or gas supply, it can stay on one leaf for days.

Early records run for 5 to 13 days on C3, CAM and facultative-CAM species. A comparison against a research-grade reference instrument is under way.

My roleMechanical design of the transparent, airtight chamber and a low-power lid mechanism. I then asked whether the switch itself could be thermoformed PETG. It could: a magnet-triggered bistable flexure that holds its state without power. That mechanism grew into BEAMbot.

With Qitong Li, Julia Wang, Dayanara Chavez, Raj Dave and Cassandra Marquez · PI: Nivedita Arora · Project page ↗

Earlier research

Westlake University · 2024–25

Biodegradable cellulose-hydrogel actuators

This cellulose film looks like PET, but its mechanics change with crosslinking and regeneration. By tuning how it is prepared, we made pneumatic chambers that deform at low pressure yet stay robust under higher loads, and that can degrade after use.

My roleI designed and prototyped the gripper, modeled its multimodal kinematics, and integrated the mechanics with electrical control for system-level tests.

Co-first authorMajor revision, Science Advances

Westlake University · 2023–24

Fabric hydraulic actuators for the deep sea

TPU-coated fabric gives up extreme strain in exchange for pressure tolerance and load capacity. The 1.2-m, four-finger arm kept a stable deformation at 10 MPa of simulated hydrostatic pressure while handling heavy objects. A hydraulic winding mechanism let its length adapt from about 0.5 to 1.2 m.

My roleI designed the 1.2-m fabric chamber and reworked how the actuators were fabricated, cutting production time by about a third and improving airtightness. I also designed the hydraulic drive and winding mechanism and ran the underwater and high-pressure grasping tests.

An earlier fabric gripper for aerial grasping

NUS (Suzhou) Research Institute · 2021–22

An origami manipulator that curls like an elephant trunk

A single-degree-of-freedom origami arm. I searched crease parameters for the largest reachable range with kinematics I wrote in MATLAB. The prototype then showed what the model had left out: PLA panels, TPU-coated fabric joints heat-pressed above 180 °C, joint clearance, and motor torque against fatigue.

My roleMy final-year project. I did the kinematics and optimization, the prototype, and the actuation and control electronics.
